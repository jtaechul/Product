Vertical 9:16 video, exactly 8 seconds, one continuous take, no cuts, no transitions.

ONE CAMERA RULE (most important): the camera looks STRAIGHT DOWN for the whole video and never tilts,
turns or looks sideways. When it moves, it moves ONLY forward along its viewing direction (straight
down), like falling face-first into what it is looking at. There is never a horizon, never a waterline
across the frame and never a side view of the water.

SETTING: an overhead view of a dark wooden table, warm dim light.

THE SCROLL: one sheet of aged parchment rolled into a single paper roll lying HORIZONTALLY across the top
of the frame, like a window blind. It unrolls from top to bottom only, never sideways. No rods.

THE CHART: thin brown ink engraving in an old atlas style that fills the whole sheet. Irregular rocky
coastlines on the left and right, a small lighthouse near the top, a few small clouds, rows of tiny
depth-sounding dots, very small wave marks, two small sailing ships, and small compass roses in the top-left
and bottom-left corners only. In the middle is one irregular, off-centre deep-sea basin drawn with uneven,
wavy contour lines. There are NO circles, NO target and NO grid, and no writing, letters or numbers anywhere,
including on the borders.

TIMELINE
0.0–1.8s  The horizontal paper roll travels downward and unrolls the sheet, revealing the chart from top
          to bottom. The paper curls slightly, then lies flat.
1.8–2.0s  The roll stops at the bottom with a tiny bounce. The whole chart is still.
2.0–3.0s  One brass magnifying glass, with no hand or fingers anywhere in the frame, slides in from the
          lower-right corner and stops over the deep-sea basin in the middle. Through the lens the ink lines
          look magnified.
3.0–3.8s  A HOLE INTO THE DEEP: the whole circle inside the lens turns into a view straight DOWN into deep,
          dark water, as if the lens were a round window cut through the paper, looking down into a
          bottomless abyss. The lens is completely full: dark teal near the rim, fading to near-black navy
          in the centre. There is no water surface, no sunlight, no ripples, no horizon and no half-filled
          water. Tiny specks drift slowly down into the depth. Outside the lens, the paper and table stay
          unchanged.
3.8–5.0s  PULLED IN: the camera is drawn smoothly and quickly straight down INTO the lens, in the same
          direction it is looking. The brass rim grows and slides out past all four edges of the frame at
          the same time, and the paper and table disappear outside it. We are now deep inside the dark
          water, still looking straight down.
5.0–6.0s  DESCENDING: the camera keeps sinking straight down into the abyss while still looking down.
          Small particles rush past, from the centre outward toward the edges of the frame, giving a
          feeling of speed. The water gets darker and darker.
6.0–8.0s  HOLD IN THE ABYSS: the descent slows smoothly to an almost complete stop. The whole frame is
          calm, very dark deep-sea navy blue, close to black at the edges, with only a few tiny pale
          particles drifting very slowly (marine snow). There is NO water surface, NO sunlight, NO light
          rays, NO shimmering light and NO bright area anywhere, especially not at the top of the frame.
          The centre of the frame is empty, clean and still. Nothing else happens.

SOUND: soft paper rustling from 0.0s to 1.8s. A faint glass-slide sound from 2.0s to 3.0s. A soft deep
water "whoomp" at 3.8s. A low muffled underwater rush from 3.9s to 6.0s. Then quiet, low deep-sea
ambience from 6.0s to 8.0s. No music. No voice.

NEVER SHOW: a water surface, sunlight, light rays or surface shimmer after 3.0s; a horizon, a waterline,
or water filling only half of the lens; the camera tilting or looking sideways; a scroll that opens
sideways; circles, a target or a grid; writing, letters or numbers; animals, fish or shadows of creatures;
hands, fingers or people; a second magnifying glass; whirlpools or spinning; bubbles or objects in the
centre during the last 2 seconds; watermarks or logos; cuts or flicker.
